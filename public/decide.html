How this is calculated
  • Selling early: the sale price minus selling costs. That money is assumed to earn your chosen rate until the later sale date, so both paths are compared at the same time.
  • Selling later: the projected price minus selling costs, rebuild costs not covered by insurance (with your overrun), and your monthly costs until the sale, with insurance premiums rising each year.
  • Break-even: the sale price at that later date where both paths come out equal.
  • Recovery speeds are assumptions: slow is −2% a year, typical +3%, fast +6%. Nobody knows which will happen.
  • Not included: rent or living costs that happen on every path, taxes, and loan details. Ask a CPA about taxes before deciding.
